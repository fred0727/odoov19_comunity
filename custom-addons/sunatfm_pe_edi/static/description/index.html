SUNAT Peru E-Invoice

Electronic invoicing tools for Odoo 19 Community and Peruvian companies that need to prepare and track SUNAT documents.

Features

  • UBL 2.1 document generation and local XSD validation.
  • XML signature support with PKCS#12 certificates.
  • SUNAT Beta transport and auditable document history.
  • Invoices, receipts, credit notes and debit notes.
  • Electronic delivery guides, vehicles and drivers.
  • Detractions, withholdings, QR codes and payment details.
  • Custom report layouts for sales, purchases and invoices.

Requirements

Odoo 19 Community with the Accounting, Peru Localization, Sales, Purchases and Inventory modules installed.

SUNAT credentials, certificates, permissions and fiscal configuration must be supplied by the customer. This module does not include credentials or a PSE/OSE subscription.

Support and compliance

Test the configuration in the SUNAT Beta environment before using it in production. The module provides technical tools and does not replace the customer's fiscal obligations or SUNAT certification.
